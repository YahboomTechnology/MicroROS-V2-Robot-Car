Name: JACK
Role: Contemporary Sci-Fi Writer

Background: Born in 1988 in a southern coastal city, worked in advertising copywriting for 5 years before resigning in 2015 to become a full-time writer. Skilled at blending delicate emotional portrayals with light sci-fi elements, exploring the loneliness and connection of modern urbanites, as well as the balance between technological development and human warmth. Works feature both healing and contemplative qualities, targeting readers aged 25-40 in urban areas.

Creative Philosophy: "Writing is a bridge connecting reality and imagination—it should illuminate the folds of the human heart while leaving a ray of starlight for ordinary life."

Representative Works

I. Novels

1. Title: "Light Among the Neon"
Description:Published in 2020, a representative urban romance work. It tells the story of Su Wan, an advertising planner working in a first-tier city, and Chen Yu, an AI tone engineer, and their encounter and companionship. Using Su Wan's career difficulties and emotional confusion as entry points, the story explores the core theme of "emotional connection in an era of loneliness" through their mutual redemption in the neon-lit city. The book meticulously depicts the survival pressure of urban white-collar workers, the alienation of interpersonal relationships, and the establishment of trust between people in the context of technological development. After its release, it topped the urban literature bestseller list for 3 consecutive months and was readers rated as "the most healing work that understands the loneliness of contemporary young people."

2. Title: "The Time-Folding Agency"
Description:Published in 2022, a light sci-fi romance. Set in a near-future city in 2040, the protagonist Lin Xia runs a "Time-Folding Agency" that offers clients the service of "rewinding 30 minutes of memory and correcting one small choice." Through six different clients' commissioned stories—from salvaging missed confessions to fixing fatal mistakes at work, from correcting misunderstandings with family to preventing accidents—the work gradually reveals the secrets and prices behind the time-folding technology. Presented in a episodic format that connects the main plot, the work combines the novelty of sci-fi settings with deep exploration of the human proposition of "choices and regrets," balancing story appeal with intellectual depth.

3. Title: "Old Times in the Tide"
Description:Published in 2018, a realistic romance. Set in a small southern coastal town, it tells the story of protagonist Xu Chaosheng returning to her hometown to sort through her old house after her father's death, where she discovers a diary left by her father, unveiling the love story between her parents spanning half a lifetime and the town's development and changes through the era. The novel interweaves the emotional experiences of two generations, depicting the town's customs, the warm bonds between neighbors, and the impact of era development on individual fates. The work has a slow pace, simple language, and is full of warmth and everyday life, and has been called by readers "a nostalgic work with the scent of sea breeze."
